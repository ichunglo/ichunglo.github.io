# Publication checklist

## Evidence

- [ ] Central claim is narrower than or equal to the evidence.
- [ ] Reported observations, derived values, inferences, and proposals are distinguishable.
- [ ] Consequential numbers have model, comparator, units, time point, and uncertainty where available.
- [ ] Primary sources are linked; preprints are labeled.
- [ ] Derived calculations are reproducible and state their assumptions.
- [ ] The strongest credible alternative explanation is addressed.
- [ ] Proposed experiments discriminate among hypotheses.
- [ ] “What would change my mind?” names realistic outcomes.

## Editorial

- [ ] Title and description are accurate and non-sensational.
- [ ] Bottom line appears before supporting detail.
- [ ] Cover image is editorial art and cannot be mistaken for data.
- [ ] Cover alt text is descriptive.
- [ ] Conflicts or material AI involvement are disclosed where relevant.
- [ ] Article date and revision history are correct.

## Site

- [ ] `quarto render` succeeds without warnings that affect publication.
- [ ] Internal and external links work.
- [ ] Article appears under the intended editorial format and scientific subjects.
- [ ] Social preview uses the correct article cover.
- [ ] RSS description and article metadata are correct.
- [ ] Public deployment has explicit author approval.
